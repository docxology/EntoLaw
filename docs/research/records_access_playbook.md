# Records-access research and review playbook

This is a reusable research workflow. It prepares structured questions and
unsent drafts from held evidence. It neither re-seeds an archive nor authorizes
agency contact. The resource ledger separates publication date, verification
date and the historical date relevant to a request. A live web reading is not
the exact-revision law corpus required by `global_law.py`.

## Route the question

Record the agency, component, custodian, jurisdiction, subject matter, date
range, known identifiers and the source supporting each. Federal FOIA is not a
state public-records law. California CPRA is not Oregon's statute. A repository
or researcher's location does not decide the agency's governing law. Unknown
or unsupported profiles retain an uncomputed legal clock; a useful research
plan can still be prepared with human legal review pending.

## Preserve the decision record

Before proposing a reply, read the operative request and entire retained
response with its attachments; record gaps explicitly. Separate transmission,
receipt, acknowledgment, determination and production. For FOIA, the reviewed
DOJ resources distinguish the determination period from prompt production,
describe routing, tolling and unusual circumstances, and identify appeal and
mediation mechanisms. Review agency regulations and the actual notice before
assigning an action date. Fee category and public-interest waiver are separate
questions, not automatic attributes of academic work.

For California, the reviewed official guidance and dated statutory compilation
distinguish the initial determination from availability of records. Consider
segregable material, existing electronic formats, and assistance in focusing a
request. Record the requested format and any actual technical limitation.
Legislative Information retrieval failed during this verification, so the dated
compilation is not certified as the current code. Check current text and local
procedures before relying on it for a deadline or remedy.

For Oregon, consult ORS 192.324, 192.329 and 192.338 for acknowledgment, response,
exceptions and separation of exempt material. The legislature's chapter page
flags amendments from its 2026 session. Its amendment table identifies changes
to the conditional-exemption section, so do not treat the chapter alone as a
fully consolidated current exemption review. This resource does not add an
Oregon computational deadline profile.

## Build a discriminating search plan

Describe existing record series rather than require an answer to a theory.
Use a scope table: custodian/component; record category; bounded dates;
identifiers; known system or series; reason for believing the record may exist;
and fallback clarification. Ask what search was performed when the response
leaves its scope unclear. Distinguish referral, component-specific search,
finding-aid search, a no-records statement and a neither-confirm-nor-deny
position. Record the agency's asserted basis without deciding its validity.

For withholding, retain the cited provision, affected record category, stated
rationale and segregation account. Federal foreseeable-harm analysis belongs
to its own statutory context; do not export it as a universal state-law test.
For fees, retain the estimate, category, requested reduction and factual basis;
for preservation, retain the applicable policy and any notice rather than
asserting that a request alone proves all retention duties.

## Advance the project

Maintain a question/evidence/alternative/record-test matrix, a source review
queue, a jurisdiction review queue and a next-action queue. Each row links to
the held request and source hashes. A source that is unread or inaccessible
remains an acquisition or reading task; it cannot supply a quotation. Scholarly
resources provide concepts and questions, independently of agency evidence.

Any justified correspondence stays in a project-declared local draft location,
begins
`DRAFT — NOT SENT`, and states its request and agency reference. A person checks
the operative law, factual predicates, attachments, links and procedural remedy
before sending. No generated status or green software gate records that review.
